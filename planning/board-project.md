# Project Kanban board

Method: Scrumban (see [`project-wbs.md`](project-wbs.md), section 2). Tool: GitHub Projects.

## Columns

Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, Done.

## Work-in-progress limits

- In Progress: at most **2 issues per member** and at most **2 people per issue** (the owner and one supporter).
- In Review: at most **3 issues** team-wide.

## Rules

- Every issue is linked to its PR and carries an iteration label: MVP, Buffer, Final-W1, Final-W2, Final-W3 or Final-W4.
- A member who finishes early first supports a critical-path or blocked issue of a teammate, then pulls new work from Ready.
- An issue moves to Done only when it meets the Definition of Done in [`docs/FEATURES.md`](../docs/FEATURES.md), section 4.
