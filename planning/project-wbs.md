## 2. Project Management Method Evaluation & Tooling

- **Why Scrumban (vs. Waterfall, pure Scrum & pure Kanban):** Waterfall was rejected because late integration cannot survive the 9-day MVP window. Pure Scrum was rejected because fixed-length sprints with a frozen sprint backlog do not fit our uneven milestones (9-day MVP track, 1-week buffer, 4-week final track). Pure Kanban was rejected because it has no timeboxes and no shared quality bar, while our milestones are hard deadlines. From Scrum we keep timeboxed iterations (Iteration 1 = MVP track Nov 16–24, the buffer week, then weekly Iterations 2–5: Dec 2–8, 9–15, 16–22, 23–30), a team-wide Definition of Done, a short planning sync at the start of each iteration, and a review plus retrospective at its end whose notes feed the post-mortem. From Kanban we keep the pull-based board, WIP limits and dynamic assignment: every issue has a default owner who is accountable for it, but work is redistributed as the project advances.

- **Board, WIP Limits & Dynamic Assignment:** Tracked in GitHub Projects (Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, Done) with mandatory issue linking and iteration labels (MVP, Buffer, Final-W1…W4). In Progress: at most 2 issues per member and at most 2 people per issue (owner + one supporter). A member who finishes an issue early first uses that capacity to support a critical-path or blocked issue of a teammate, pairing with its owner, before pulling new work from Ready. In Review: at most 3 issues team-wide, so reviews are cleared before new work starts.

- **Definition of Done:** Every issue meets the team-wide DoD defined in the Feature Prioritisation document (section 4) before moving to Done: cross-layer review, green CI, success- and failure-path tests, updated openapi.yaml and /about.json, EN/ES i18n keys, no new critical accessibility issues, no secrets in logs, docs/ADR updated, and a clean docker compose up --build. Catalogue features additionally require every catalogue acceptance criterion to be observable.

- **OpenAPI-First Contract Governance:** openapi.yaml (M-05) is derived directly from the domain/node schemas (M-07, M-09, M-10) by Day 4, generating typed mocks (MSW) so React Web and React Native Android progress in parallel without blocking.

- **Branch Protection, CI/CD & Automated Deployment:** Protected main branch requiring feature branches (feat/<id>-<slug>), mandatory cross-layer peer review, green CI (golangci-lint, ESLint, tsc, unit/contract tests, Gitleaks, Trivy, govulncheck, npm audit), and an automated CD workflow deploying the Docker Compose stack without manual steps.
